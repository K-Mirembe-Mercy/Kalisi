THE NEW SANCTUARY — IN PROGRESS

Building the House

We are building — the same way our faith is built. Brick by brick, together.

Behind every service under the tent, a new sanctuary is rising from the red soil of Kalisizo. It has no roof yet. The walls are still bare brick. And every week, this congregation gathers inside its unfinished frame to give thanks for how far they've already come — Ebenezer, "thus far the Lord has helped us."

  • 01

    Foundation & walls

    Brickwork for the main sanctuary is complete, raised by members and local artisans.

  • 02

    Roof trusses

    Timber trusses are going up, frame by frame, ahead of roofing sheets.

  • 03

    Finishing & furnishing

    Plastering, flooring, seating, and sound — the next phase of the build.
