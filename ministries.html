Ministries & Services

Where to plug in.

From weekly gatherings to healing prayer and outreach, here is how our church family serves Kalisizo — and how you can join in.

Weekly Services

Gatherings focused on prayer, fellowship, and prophetic ministry.

SUNDAY · 9:00 AM

Main Worship Service

Our central gathering — praise, the Word taught by Pastor Lubega William, and prayer for healing over the whole congregation.

WEDNESDAY · 5:30 PM

Midweek Prayer & Fellowship

A smaller, intimate gathering built around prayer, prophetic ministry, and encouragement between neighbors.

FRIDAY · 7:00 PM

Night of Miracles

A dedicated night of healing and deliverance prayer, standing in faith together for spiritual and physical restoration.

Ministry Focus

Miracle & Healing Ministry

At the center of our church is a strong emphasis on looking to God for both spiritual and physical restoration. Every week, members bring their burdens forward — sickness, grief, financial hardship, broken relationships — and the congregation stands together in prayer, believing for breakthrough.

This ministry is not a program on the side; it's woven through everything we do, from Sunday altar calls to our dedicated Friday Night of Miracles.
