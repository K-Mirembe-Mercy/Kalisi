Pastor Lubega William

His Ministry

A pastor shaped by the community he serves.

Pastor Lubega William leads Ebenezer Miracle Center Church in the heart of Kalisizo Town Council, where his teaching centers on biblical truth applied to everyday life. Under his leadership, the church has grown into a spiritual beacon for the local community — a place dedicated to faith, healing, and personal transformation through the Gospel.

His ministry carries a strong emphasis on prayer and prophetic ministry, with weekly gatherings built around hearing from God together, and a Miracle & Healing Ministry that looks to God for both spiritual and physical restoration. Beyond the pulpit, he leads outreach initiatives supporting families and youth across the region, and is currently guiding the congregation through the construction of a new permanent sanctuary — building the house the same patient way he has built the church: brick by brick, together.
